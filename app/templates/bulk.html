How it works

  1. POST /upload/jobs — create the job, get pre-signed URLs.
  2. PUT <pre-signed URL> — upload each document.
  3. POST /upload/jobs/{id}/actions/complete — trigger ingestion.
  4. GET /upload/jobs/{id} — poll job status.

Agreement Manager then runs AI extraction and creates an agreement record for each document.
